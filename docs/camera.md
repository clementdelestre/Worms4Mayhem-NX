Removed as not W4M: the 1 s chase of every blast point (`Controls::impact`), hp-count tracks re-requested within 200 ms or for a worm already in clear view, the end of a death / count track without its RestTime, the flight to the new worm at turn start, the flight back to the worm after an event camera. The axis-cross test now takes the object's velocity only above W4M's 1e-5 u²/ms² (0.16 m/s), so a bobbing drowned worm falls back to the event direction.

W4M's lost test is the plain land segment camera → object (0x51abf0 → 0x466a20 → 0x4661a0: the whole segment, a hit anywhere before its end, no radius, flags 0); the object is the payload's own position (Radius 5 units keeps it off the land at contact); nothing skips it near the event point or close to impact, and one frame is enough (0x532589). Ours stops the segment 0.5 m short, so it is if anything more lenient.

PiP: an event camera served during the active worm's turn is the inset (CPU shots included: the AI standing still changes nothing), the main view the worm's; it grows when the turn ends. The former rule (the inset only while the worm moves with retreat time left, from +0x2c0) read the camera-type flag as the drawn camera (docs/camera-w4m.md §11.6b).

PiP left open: a camera served while the inset slides off is never drawn in W4M (PiP.GoneOffScreen drops +0x2b0, 0x5228c5); ours slides a new inset on once the old one is gone [ours]. Every PiP move starts where the inset is (`pip.from`), as PiPService's from values (0x636590).

Near-vertical views: `level()` (controls.cpp, end of `Controls::camera`, on the drawn view and the PiP) eases `up` toward the horizontal
heading when the view axis is within ~20° of vertical, (0,1,0) on NaN [ours]: the blend in `present()` keeps up (0,1,0) while position and
target ease, so a view swept through the vertical (Blimp looking down, a falling crate or donkey filmed from above) made a degenerate lookAt
that rolled 90° / 180°. W4M's handling there is not traced. Scene cameras (`persp`) are set after it and keep their node up.

HP count of several worms [disasm, 0x5abeec / 0x51d5ad]: each hurt worm's display sends its own WormTrackCamera request at the same ApplyDamage instant; the one pending slot keeps the best (higher priority, or a 5 reaching the water sooner) and is emptied after every CMS update, so one worm is filmed and the other requests are lost. Ours: `ask()` per `countGroup` worm (controls.cpp `watch`).

## Aim view FOV and worm hiding [ours, follows camera-w4m.md]
- Aim camera position: the worm's feet + `Game::EYE` 0.75 m (Worm.EyeLevelOffset 15 units), straight over the worm, where every shot leaves (`Controls::eye`, `launchPoint`, `muzzle`); the reticle point and the focus are on `eye + aimDir * AIM_FOCUS` [disasm 0x528ed1, camera-w4m.md §11.3a]. It was the worm's centre pushed 0.5 m back along the aim, 0.25 m lower at level aim.
- Held weapon in the aim view [ours, camera-w4m.md §11.3a]: with the camera inside the worm and no scope, the held mesh alone is redrawn over the scene (own near plane 0.05, depth cleared) at its true `WeaponLocator` matrix from the aim clip, seen from the exact eye; no scale or offset. The real game draws the worm's arm too and shows the Bazooka on the right third (observed); the cause is not traced, so ours differs there (the Bazooka sits nearer the centre, the arm is missing, hand-held meshes such as the Grenade are mostly out of view). It was a view-model at (0.4, -0.32, 0.7) m from the eye, scaled 0.4 (invented). The worm's body is not drawn (hidden worm, below).
- Aim, fine aim and scope all use the CMS default projection (`Controls::FOV0` = 2·atan(0.48) = 51.28° vertical, 0x4d8067 [disasm]), times the HeadCam zoom [disasm 0x51e150]; the HeadCam zoom is the player's (zoom keys, L / R on the pad, user-requested 2026-10-04), CAMTWK Camera.Head.MinZoom 0.05 .. MaxZoom 1 [data], kept through the turn, reset when the turn leaves Aim (user-requested; W4M keeps it in a global never reset that we found), zoom keys ÷ / × Camera.Head.ZoomSpeed 1.1 per CMS update (100 a second), wheel − MouseZoomSpeed 0.08 (HeadCam 0x528e23..0x528e90) [disasm, data]; the Blimp: × / ÷ Camera.Blimp.ZoomSpeed 0.99 per update (0x52a5e0); binoculars keep their scout zoom. The current worm is hidden once the aim camera is inside it (1.2 m) [ours]: the HeadCam alpha 0x5a446b, max(0, 1 - |v|^2/0.016), read as the worm alpha left it drawn in front of the scope (2026-10-03). That alpha is the active worm's whole graphic (WXWormGraphicEntity 0x5a4420, only for [0x95fb04], set through 0x61fe90 vfunc +0x24); under any other camera it is clamp((d − Camera.WormTransparencyDist 25) / (Camera.WormOpaqueDist 50 − 25)), d the camera distance in units [disasm, data], our `Xray::opacity` (1.25 .. 2.5 m).
